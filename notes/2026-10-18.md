## What happened when I ran it

```text
python validate.py            ->  30 items, 0 errors (see output for warnings)
python evaluate.py --backend mock --langs hi
```

- `validate.py` passes: unique ids, no duplicate prompts, all enums valid, every item sourced.
- The `mock` backend runs the full pipeline end-to-end and writes `results/mock/summary.md`.
  **These are synthetic answers — not results.** The value today is that the *pipeline* works.

## What I learned / decisions made

- **Open-ended safety items need an empty `reference`.** The schema originally required a
  non-empty reference for everything; I relaxed it so `reference` may be `""` for `rubric` items,
  while `exact_match`/`reference_based` still require one. Encoded in `validate.py`.
- **Exact-match scoring needed aliases.** A model may answer `B`, `(B)`, or `किताब`; the `accept`
  field handles this without weakening the check.
- **chrF is enough for translation v1**, but a judge layered on top is the real research question
  (paper #2: judge reliability in Indian languages).
